# Hangman word list

`hangman_fr.json` is generated by `build_hangman_words.py` from **Lexique 3.83**
(<http://www.lexique.org>, Boris New & Christophe Pallier), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The word list is a
derived work, shared under the same **CC BY-SA 4.0** license; the rest of this
repository keeps its own license.

References:

- New, B., Pallier, C., Brysbaert, M., & Ferrand, L. (2004). Lexique 2: A new French
  lexical database. *Behavior Research Methods, Instruments, & Computers*, 36(3), 516–524.
- New, B., Brysbaert, M., Veronis, J., & Pallier, C. (2007). The use of film subtitles to
  estimate word frequencies. *Applied Psycholinguistics*, 28(4), 661–677.

Changes made to Lexique: only common nouns in their base form (not plural), whose
most frequent use is as a noun, 6 to 12 letters without accents, letters only,
frequency of at least 3 per million words; words of `blocklist_fr.txt` removed;
grouped by frequency into three levels (easy ≥ 50, medium 10–50, hard 3–10 per million).

Rebuild after changing the blocklist:

```bash
curl -LO http://www.lexique.org/databases/Lexique383/Lexique383.zip
unzip Lexique383.zip Lexique383/Lexique383.tsv
python3 build_hangman_words.py Lexique383/Lexique383.tsv
```
